6 · Learning Nugget

Bear steepener. When long-term yields rise faster than short-term yields, steepening the curve — it usually signals the market pricing in more inflation or term premium rather than growth optimism. It hits long-duration assets hardest, which is why high-multiple tech and long bonds are the first to wobble when it happens.
