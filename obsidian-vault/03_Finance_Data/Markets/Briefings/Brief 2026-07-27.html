One thing to sound smart about

The Mag 7 selloff wasn't about earnings — it was the market switching its scorecard from EPS to free cash flow. Alphabet (capex guide up to ~$205B) and Tesla now run negative FCF because of AI spend, and "no one knows the ROI." The tell: Mag 7's valuation premium to the S&P has compressed toward ~10% from 30%+, the cheapest in over a decade. Translation — the cheap-vs-history crowd and the FCF crowd are now looking at the same names and disagreeing violently. That's why AAPL (positive FCF, at highs) and the capex-heavy platforms are decoupling.

Learning nugget

Free cash flow (FCF) = operating cash flow − capex. A company can post rising GAAP earnings while FCF goes negative if it's plowing cash into buildings and chips faster than the business throws it off — which is exactly the AI-capex trap investors just started pricing in.
