5 · One Thing To Sound Smart About

Nvidia's reported ~$250B backstop for OpenAI's Ohio data-center debt (plus up to $350B in GPU-purchase financing) is reviving "circular financing" fears: NVDA effectively lends its own credit so a non-investment-grade customer can buy NVDA chips. Michael Burry disclosed a bigger NVDA short — "around and around we go." The contrarian read: this props up reported GPU demand while shifting real credit risk onto Nvidia's balance sheet — the tell that the AI capex cycle is maturing from cash-funded to debt-funded, exactly the phase that broke dot-com telecom.

6 · Learning Nugget

Vendor financing. When a supplier funds its customers' purchases of its own products, current revenue looks stronger than underlying demand and the supplier quietly absorbs the customers' credit risk. It flattered late-1990s telecom (Lucent, Nortel) right before the bust — which is why the Nvidia–OpenAI structure is drawing scrutiny.
