6 · Learning Nugget

Bear steepener

A bear steepener is when long-term yields rise faster than short-term ones, steepening the curve in a “bearish” (falling-bond-price) way. It usually signals rising inflation expectations or a fatter term premium — extra compensation for duration and fiscal risk — rather than optimism about growth, and it hits long-duration assets like tech stocks and mortgages hardest.
