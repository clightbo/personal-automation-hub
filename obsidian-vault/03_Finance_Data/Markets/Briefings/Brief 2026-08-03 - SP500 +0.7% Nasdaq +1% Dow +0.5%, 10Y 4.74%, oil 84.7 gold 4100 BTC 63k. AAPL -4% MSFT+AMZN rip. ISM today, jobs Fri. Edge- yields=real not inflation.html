6 · Learning Nugget

Real rates vs. nominal. A nominal Treasury yield equals the real yield plus expected inflation (the "breakeven"). When yields rise because of real rates rather than breakevens, it signals the market expects tighter-for-longer policy and resilient growth — and that compresses equity valuations more than an equal-sized, inflation-driven move would.
