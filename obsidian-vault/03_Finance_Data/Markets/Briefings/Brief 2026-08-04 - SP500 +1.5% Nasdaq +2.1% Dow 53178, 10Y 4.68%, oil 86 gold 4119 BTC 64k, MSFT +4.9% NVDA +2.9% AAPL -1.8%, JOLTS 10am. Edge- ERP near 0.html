One Thing To Sound Smart About

The Edge

The equity risk premium is near zero. The S&P's forward earnings yield now sits roughly level with the 10Y Treasury (an ERP of ~0.02% by one measure — among the lowest on record). Translation: at record highs, stocks pay you almost nothing extra over risk-free bonds — right as the Fed is ~81% likely to hike in September. You're not being compensated for the risk you're taking. (Damodaran; SimpleVisor / RIA)

Learning Nugget

Equity Risk Premium (ERP)

The extra return investors demand for holding stocks over risk-free Treasuries, commonly proxied as (forward earnings yield − 10Y yield). When it compresses toward zero, you're paid almost nothing extra to bear equity risk — historically a marker of stretched valuations and thin downside cushion.
