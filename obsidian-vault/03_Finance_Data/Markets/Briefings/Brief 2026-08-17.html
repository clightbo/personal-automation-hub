6 · Learning Nugget

Stagflation. It's the rare combination of stagnant growth and high inflation at the same time. It's the Fed's nightmare because its main tool — interest rates — pulls in opposite directions: cut to support growth and you fuel inflation; hike to fight inflation and you deepen the slowdown.
