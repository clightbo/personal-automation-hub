## 5 · One Thing to Sound Smart About

If stagflation is the tail risk, **credit is where it shows up first — and credit is priced for perfection.** US high-yield OAS sits around **281 bps**, in the richest decile of its history against a long-run median near ~450, with the VIX around 16. Both markets price near-flawless outcomes, which is asymmetric: spreads can barely tighten but can gap wide on any shock. HY-OAS/VIX divergences have front-run several past drawdowns — so the contrarian read is that the cheapest hedge right now isn't equity vol, it's credit. Watch the first crack in HY, not the VIX.

## 6 · Learning Nugget

**Stagflation** is the rare combination of stagnant growth and high inflation at once. It's the Fed's nightmare because its main tool — interest rates — pulls in opposite directions: cut to support growth and you fuel inflation; hike to fight inflation and you deepen the slowdown.
