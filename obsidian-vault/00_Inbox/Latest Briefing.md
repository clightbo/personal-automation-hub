## 5 · One Thing to Sound Smart About

The Treasury curve is un-inverting through a **bear steepener** — long yields rising on **term premium**, not growth optimism — and this is now the third oil-driven curve episode of 2026 (April Hormuz, June payrolls, July). The subtle tell: if Thursday's CPI runs hot, check **where**. A headline spike from energy is supply-side and shouldn't, by the textbook, force a hike; **core** (a tame 2.5% in July) is what should drive policy. A Fed that hikes into an oil supply shock risks a policy error — watch 5s30s steepening and the long bond as the market's "policy-mistake" barometer.

## 6 · Learning Nugget

**Bear steepener** — when long-term yields rise faster than short-term ones, steepening the curve in a "bearish" (falling-bond-price) way. It usually signals rising inflation expectations or a fatter term premium (compensation for duration and fiscal risk) rather than growth optimism, and it hits long-duration assets like tech stocks and mortgages hardest.
