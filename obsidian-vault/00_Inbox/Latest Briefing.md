## 5 · One Thing To Sound Smart About

Nvidia's reported **~$250B backstop for OpenAI's** Ohio data-center debt (plus up to $350B in GPU-purchase financing) is reviving **"circular financing"** fears: NVDA effectively lends its own credit so a non-investment-grade customer can buy NVDA chips. Michael Burry disclosed a larger NVDA short — _"around and around we go."_ The contrarian read: this props up *reported* GPU demand while shifting real credit risk onto Nvidia's balance sheet — the tell that the AI capex cycle is maturing from cash-funded to debt-funded, the same phase that broke dot-com telecom. (Source: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/nvidia-s-750-billion-deals-revive-fear-of-ai-circular-financing), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/27/nvidias-potential-250b-backstop-for-openai-is-another-strike-against-the-ai-trade.html))

## 6 · Learning Nugget

**Vendor financing.** When a supplier funds its customers' purchases of its own products, current revenue looks stronger than underlying demand and the supplier quietly absorbs the customers' credit risk. It flattered late-1990s telecom (Lucent, Nortel) right before the bust — which is why the Nvidia–OpenAI structure is drawing scrutiny.
