## 3 · Top 3 Things That Matter Today

1. **The Fed is now debating a HIKE, not a cut.** Aug payrolls came in hot (~160K, well above forecast), pushing fed-funds futures to ~61% odds of a 25bp increase Sep 15–16 (from ~50% the prior day). It repriced the whole curve — 10Y back to ~4.79%.
2. **Oil shock from renewed US–Iran conflict.** Brent ~$97 (+9% on the week) after Iran struck Kuwait and Hormuz shipping risk jumped. An energy shock on top of hot jobs is textbook stagflation pressure.
3. **CPI Friday is the whole ballgame.** With a live hike on the table, Fri Sep 11 CPI (and Thu PPI) is the last major inflation read before the FOMC. Hot cements the hike; soft defuses it.

## 5 · One Thing to Sound Smart About

A Fed hike with the S&P ~1% from all-time highs is genuinely rare. Markets are pricing a coin-flip-to-likely rate *increase* while equities sit near records — a late-cycle "hawkish surprise" under Chair Kevin Warsh, whose Jackson Hole remarks already lifted term premium (a bear-steepener: 10Y ~4.79%, 30Y north of 5%). Stocks are treating good news as bad news again — a correlation flip that historically precedes higher volatility. Contrarian angle: an Iran-driven oil shock *plus* a hot CPI could be the catalyst that finally cracks a credit market priced to perfection (HY spreads compressed toward ~280bp). Watch spreads, not just stocks. _(Source: Roic / CNBC.)_

## 6 · Learning Nugget

**Bear steepener** — when long-term yields rise *faster* than short-term yields, steepening the curve. It typically signals the market pricing in more inflation or term premium rather than growth optimism, and it hits long-duration assets (high-multiple tech, long bonds) hardest.
