6 · Learning Nugget

Bear steepener

A "bear steepener" is when long-term yields rise faster than short-term yields, steepening the curve as bond prices fall. It typically signals the market pricing in more inflation, heavier government borrowing, or a higher term premium — rather than expectations that the Fed will hike the short-term policy rate.
