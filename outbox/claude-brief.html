Learning Nugget

Concept: The MOVE Index The MOVE index is the bond market's version of the VIX — it measures expected volatility in U.S. Treasuries. When it's low (like today's ~74.7) it signals calm rates that support tight credit spreads and risk appetite, so a sudden spike in MOVE often precedes or accompanies equity drawdowns.
