The Angle

Stocks are near records while the 10-year sits above 5%. That only works if earnings keep rising, so the weeks ahead are a test of profits against rates, with inflation data as the referee.

One New Thing

Market concentration. When a few companies make up a large share of an index, the index behaves like a bet on those companies. Over 21% in three stocks means a bad week for them can drag the whole S&P down, even if most other stocks are fine.
