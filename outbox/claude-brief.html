6 · Learning Nugget

Term premium is the extra yield investors demand for holding a long bond instead of rolling short ones — compensation for duration and inflation risk, separate from expected rate moves. When the 10Y falls even as rate-cut odds don't change, a shrinking term premium is often doing the work, which flatters equity valuations by lowering the discount rate.
