The Angle

With little data on the calendar, the market is left trading two variables: oil and yields. Stocks have shown they can rally on rate-hike relief, but a 5.27% 10-year and $100 Brent are a hard ceiling on valuations if either moves higher.

One New Thing

What are FOMC minutes? They are a detailed record of a Fed meeting, released three weeks later. They show how divided policymakers were and which risks they discussed, which can move markets even though the decision itself is already known.
