6 · Learning Nugget

Duration risk. A bond's price falls more for a given rise in yields the longer its maturity — that's duration. It's also why high-multiple tech (whose cash flows sit far in the future) trades like a "long-duration" asset and sells off hardest when rates jump, exactly as QQQ did versus the Dow yesterday.
