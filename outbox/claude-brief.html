6 · Learning Nugget

Option-Adjusted Spread (OAS). It's the yield premium a corporate bond pays over Treasuries after stripping out the value of embedded options (like a call feature), so you're comparing pure credit risk. A very tight OAS means investors are demanding little compensation to take credit risk — often a late-cycle signal that complacency is high.
