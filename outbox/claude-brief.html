6 · Learning Nugget

Supply-shock stagflation. When a negative supply shock (an oil spike) raises prices and cuts growth, the Fed faces a real dilemma: hiking to fight inflation deepens the slowdown, cutting to support growth entrenches inflation. That's why an oil shock can push bond yields up even as stocks fall — breaking the usual "stocks down = bonds up" hedge that most portfolios rely on.
