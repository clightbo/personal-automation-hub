6 · Learning Nugget

Term premium. It's the extra yield investors demand to hold a long bond instead of rolling short-term bills — when the 10Y rises on term premium rather than rate-hike expectations, it's signaling fiscal/supply-of-bonds worry, not a stronger economy. That distinction matters today: a yield backup driven by oil-inflation and issuance fears is more bearish for stocks than one driven by growth.
