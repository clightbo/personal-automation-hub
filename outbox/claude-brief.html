Oil, not the Fed, is now the swing factor: the central bank has already hiked and signaled little appetite for more, so a crude spike is what could change that and keep the 10-year pinned above 5%.

One New Thing

Real yield. The yield on a Treasury after subtracting expected inflation. When it rises, non-yielding assets like gold and long-duration stocks look less attractive, which is why gold fell even as geopolitical risk grew.
