6 · Learning Nugget

Term premium is the extra yield investors demand to hold a long-dated bond instead of rolling short-term bills — compensation for inflation and rate uncertainty over time. When it rises, long yields can climb even if the market expects the Fed to eventually cut, which is exactly the disconnect playing out now.
