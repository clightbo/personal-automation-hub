6 Learning Nugget

Bear steepener: when long-term yields rise faster than short-term ones, steepening the curve. It typically signals the market pricing in more term premium, supply, or inflation risk — rather than imminent Fed cuts — and it hits long-duration assets (growth stocks, long bonds) the hardest.
