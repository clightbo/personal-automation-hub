6 · Learning Nugget — Real Yields

Real yield = nominal Treasury yield minus expected inflation (proxied by TIPS). When real yields rise yet gold also rises, it's often a signal of demand for inflation or tail-risk hedges rather than economic confidence — because gold normally weakens as real yields climb and cash becomes more attractive.
