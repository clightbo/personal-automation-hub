August PCE cools more than expected, but not enough to relax

The Angle

Good inflation news is meeting a bond market that is not in a forgiving mood. When the 10-year sits above 5.2% and the 30-year at 2002 highs, even a cooler PCE print buys equities only a modest bounce. The next leg depends on whether yields, not just inflation data, start to come down.

One New Thing

Core PCE differs from CPI in what it counts and how it weights it. PCE covers a broader basket, including spending made on consumers' behalf such as employer-paid health care, and it adjusts weights as people substitute between goods. That is one reason the Fed prefers it, and why it can run below CPI.
