Worth Noting

The Angle

Strong AI earnings are no longer enough. TSMC's record profit did not lift chips, which suggests the market is repricing the AI trade against a higher discount rate, not doubting demand.

One New Thing

PPI. The Producer Price Index measures what businesses receive for goods and services, so it tracks cost pressure earlier in the supply chain. A hot PPI after a hot CPI would suggest consumer prices have more room to rise.
