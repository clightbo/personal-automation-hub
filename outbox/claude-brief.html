Learning Nugget — Nonfarm Productivity

Nonfarm productivity measures output per hour worked; when it rises faster than wages, unit labor costs fall, letting companies pay more without stoking inflation. That's why the Fed watches it closely — strong productivity is the rare data point that can be both pro-growth and disinflationary at the same time.
