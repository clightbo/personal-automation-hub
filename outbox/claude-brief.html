6 · Learning Nugget

CONCEPT

Breakeven inflation rate. It's the yield gap between a nominal Treasury and the same-maturity TIPS, and it tells you the inflation rate the bond market is pricing in over that horizon. When an oil shock hits, watch 5-yr breakevens: if they jump, the market thinks the Fed will let inflation run; if they stay anchored, it trusts the Fed to hold the line even at the cost of growth.
