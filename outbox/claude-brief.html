Why nowG7 nations agreed to release 100 million barrels from emergency reserves, pushing WTI to about $89, yet Brent holds above $102 because disruptions around the Strait of Hormuz keep seaborne crude costly. Energy prices feed straight into inflation expectations and yields.

Worth Noting

The Angle

Good news on the economy is now bad news for bonds and vice versa. With few data releases, the market is trading two variables, yields and oil, and Friday's record Nasdaq depends on both staying calm.

One New Thing

Brent vs. WTI. Brent is the seaborne global benchmark and WTI is the US inland one. When shipping routes like Hormuz are threatened, Brent gains a risk premium over WTI, which is why they can move in opposite directions.
