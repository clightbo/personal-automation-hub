Worth Noting

The Angle

A one-day bounce after a 4.5% weekly drop proves little. What matters this week is whether earnings calls give investors enough evidence of AI returns to justify spending at a 5% cost of money. Yields, not just profits, are setting the price of growth stocks.

One New Thing

Sanctions waivers and oil. When a government eases sanctions on an oil exporter, it does not add barrels overnight, but traders price the expected extra supply immediately. That is why crude can fall on a policy headline before a single extra barrel ships.
