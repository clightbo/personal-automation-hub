6 · Learning Nugget

Bear flattener. When short-term yields rise faster than long-term yields — usually because the market expects near-term rate hikes — the yield curve "flattens" from the front end. It's called a bear flattener because rising rates pressure bond prices, and it typically signals tightening financial conditions rather than the growth optimism of a "bull steepener."
