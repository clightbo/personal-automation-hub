Worth Noting

The Angle

Stocks and yields are rising together, which usually means investors think growth is strong enough to absorb higher rates. If oil keeps falling, that bet gets easier; if it reverses, bonds become the problem.

One New Thing

Term premium. The extra yield investors demand for holding a long bond instead of rolling short ones. When it rises, long yields climb even if the Fed's path is unchanged.
