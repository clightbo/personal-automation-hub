Worth Noting

The Angle

This week stacks the two things that decide whether stocks can hold records with yields above 5%: strong bank earnings and an inflation print. Good earnings plus a tame core CPI supports the rally; a hot core number would hit the same rate worries that dented tech last week.

One New Thing

Headline vs. core inflation. Headline CPI includes volatile food and energy; core strips them out. Central banks care more about core because it shows whether price pressure is spreading. An oil-driven jump in headline CPI can look scary yet be short-lived if core stays calm.
