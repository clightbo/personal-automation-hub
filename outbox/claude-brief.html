Worth Noting

The Angle

For two years AI stocks were driven by earnings and chip supply. This week they were driven by the cost of money. When oil, inflation and a 5% Treasury yield all stay high together, the long-dated growth stories that funded the boom get repriced first, which is why the Dow can hold up on a week the Nasdaq loses 4.5%.

One New Thing

Equity duration. Like a bond, a stock's value depends on cash flows far in the future, and the further out they are, the more a rise in interest rates shrinks their present value. High-growth tech is "long-duration"; banks and consumer staples are "short-duration", which is why higher yields hit them differently.
