One Thing To Sound Smart About

Everyone will call this "risk-off," but the internals say otherwise. In a true growth scare, Treasuries and gold rally together as money flees to safety. Right now both are falling alongside stocks. That signature — bonds down, gold down, oil up — is an inflation / term-premium shock, not a flight to safety. The market isn't afraid of recession; it's repricing the odds that the Fed can't cut, and may even hike, while an oil-supply shock reheats inflation. Translation: this is stagflation-lite positioning, and the usual "buy the dip because bonds will hedge you" playbook is quietly broken this week.
edge: bonds + gold selling with stocks = inflation shock, not a growth scare — your bond hedge isn't working

Learning Nuggetterm premium

Term premium is the extra yield investors demand to hold a long bond instead of rolling short-term bills — compensation for inflation and supply uncertainty over the years ahead. When it rises (like now), long yields can climb to multi-decade highs even if expected Fed policy barely changes — which is exactly why the 10Y is breaking out while the rate-cut debate rages on.
