Hormuz reopening hopes keep oil sliding, unwinding the war-risk premium

Why nowU.S. and Iranian negotiators are reportedly exploring a phased deal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz and lift a blockade on Iranian ports, sending WTI toward $93/bbl and Brent back near $105 — well off the 52-week high of $117.63 hit during this year's Iran conflict. Iran's foreign minister has floated reopening the strait and restarting nuclear talks with Washington within seven days if the U.S. accepts its conditions.
