Worth Noting

The Angle

Thursday's index loss overstated the damage to the average stock: the equal-weight S&P 500 rose while the cap-weighted index fell. The market is being driven by a handful of AI-exposed giants, and when they stumble the headline numbers look much worse than breadth suggests. Apple's new high shows investors are still paying for earnings they trust.

One New Thing

Equal-weight vs. cap-weight. A cap-weighted index like the S&P 500 lets the biggest companies dominate its moves, while an equal-weight version gives every stock the same influence. When the two diverge, as they did Thursday, the move is about a few large names rather than the whole market.
