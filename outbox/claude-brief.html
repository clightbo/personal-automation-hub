5 · One Thing to Sound Smart About

Gold fell while oil and geopolitics jumped — that divergence is the whole story. In a classic fear shock, gold and oil rise together. This weekend gold dropped toward a one-week low even as Brent broke $90 on the Iran strike. The tell: this isn't primarily a fear bid, it's a real-yields move. A hawkish Warsh Fed pushing real rates up raises the opportunity cost of holding zero-yield gold, and that force is overpowering the safe-haven bid. Translation for your circle: watch real yields (10Y TIPS), not the VIX, to know whether this selloff has legs.

6 · Learning Nugget

Real yield = nominal Treasury yield minus expected inflation (proxied by TIPS). When real yields rise, non-cash-flowing assets like gold and long-duration growth stocks get cheaper on a relative basis — which is exactly why gold and rate-sensitive tech can fall together even during a geopolitical scare.
