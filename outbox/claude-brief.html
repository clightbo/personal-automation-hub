5 · One Thing to Sound Smart About

The Edge

Watch the capex-to-depreciation gap. The four US hyperscalers bought ~$434B of property & equipment in the four quarters through March 2026, but booked only ~$149B of depreciation. Reported earnings look great because that spend is expensed slowly over assumed 5–6 year server lives. If real useful lives are closer to 3–4 years (the Michael Burry argument), today's EPS is being flattered and 2027–28 faces a depreciation air-pocket. The contrarian tell isn't the AI headlines — it's whether that $285B gap starts closing.

6 · Learning Nugget

Concept: Useful-Life & Depreciation
Lengthening the assumed useful life of an asset lowers annual depreciation and raises reported earnings — with zero change to actual cash flow. It's why the gap between capex (cash out now) and D&A (expensed later) is a leading tell on whether a capital-heavy sector's margins are real or borrowed from the future.
