The Angle

Today is unusually clean risk-on: stocks, bitcoin and chip names all up together while yields, oil and gold all ease at once — normally at least one of those moves against the group. The single thread that could break it is Goolsbee's speech today; he's the one policymaker on record worried inflation isn't cooling as fast as this rally assumes.

One New Thing

Aggregate tariff rate: when headlines say China's tariff rate will "stay near 20%," that's a blended average across thousands of product categories, not one flat number. Negotiators fight over which specific goods get carved into a duty-relief basket (like the ~$30B one on the table this week) because moving a single high-volume category in or out can shift the effective average without changing the headline rate at all.
