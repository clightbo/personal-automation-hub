The Angle

Futures are rallying into the summit while bonds and gold aren't buying the "all clear" — that split rarely holds. Either yields catch down to the trade-deal story, or stocks catch down to the yield/oil story. Watch which one breaks first after Thursday, not the headline itself.

One New Thing

Chokepoint risk premium: oil traders price a separate "war risk" premium on top of ordinary supply/demand whenever a shipping chokepoint is threatened, because shippers reroute before a barrel is actually lost. That's why crude jumps on an attack that stops zero production — and falls back just as fast once routes look safe again.
