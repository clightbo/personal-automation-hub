The Angle

Two very different risks are competing for the market's attention: a macro squeeze from oil-fed inflation and 5%-plus yields, and an AI-sector question mark about how much capital the leading labs are burning. Monday's tape showed both can hit at once, yet Microsoft and Nvidia still rose, so the market is still rewarding cash-rich AI incumbents over the labs that depend on them for funding.

One New Thing

Apple and Nvidia together now exceed 15% of the S&P 500's weight, a record for any two stocks. That means the index's fate is increasingly a two-stock story: a passive S&P 500 holder is effectively making a large bet on the fortunes of just Apple and Nvidia, a concentration worth remembering when SPY moves.
