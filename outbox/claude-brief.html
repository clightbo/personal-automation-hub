5 · One Thing to Sound Smart About

"Bad news is good news" is back — but on a jobs report that was weak for the wrong reasons. Unemployment fell to 4.1% not from hiring but because the labor force shrank; payrolls actually contracted and two months were revised down 103k. A shrinking workforce plus softening demand is the recipe for an H2 earnings-estimate problem, not a clean soft landing. The real tell isn't CPI — it's credit spreads: equal-weight and small-cap leadership has historically unwound the moment spreads widen. That's where the first crack shows.

6 · Learning Nugget

Watch the revisions, not just the headline. BLS restates prior months as more employer surveys arrive, so a benign top-line number can hide a deteriorating trend — this month's −103k two-month revision effectively erased jobs the market had already counted. That's why economists track the 3-month average payroll trend rather than any single print.
