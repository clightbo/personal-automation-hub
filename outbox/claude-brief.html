Learning Nugget

Duration risk in equities: long-duration growth stocks (think mega-cap tech) derive most of their value from far-future cash flows, which get discounted harder when yields rise. That's the mechanical reason a jump in the 10-year hits the Nasdaq and names like AAPL more than it hits value/cyclical stocks.
