Worth Noting

The Angle

The market is treating bad inflation news as a reason to rotate, not to flee. With the Dow up 1% on a day PPI came in at more than double forecasts, investors seem to be pricing a stagflation-style regime where value and defensives beat long-duration AI growth.

One New Thing

Basis point. One hundredth of a percentage point. A move in the 10-year from 5.19% to 5.09% is 10 basis points, and traders use the unit because small moves in yields reprice trillions of dollars of bonds and stock valuations.
