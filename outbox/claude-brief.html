05 One Thing To Sound Smart About

With stocks at records and oil collapsing, the melt-up is being underwritten by the tightest high-yield credit spreads of the cycle — even as idiosyncratic default and refinancing risk quietly builds. The tell to watch isn't the VIX; it's HY spreads. If they start widening while equities keep rising, that's the textbook late-cycle divergence that front-runs risk-off. Right now they're calm — which is exactly why the first move matters. (Source: LPL 2026 Fixed Income Outlook.)

06 Learning Nugget

Credit spread — the extra yield investors demand to hold a corporate bond over a same-maturity Treasury, compensating for default risk. Because credit markets often flinch before equities do, widening spreads are a classic early-warning gauge that stock investors ignore at their peril.
