The Angle

Leadership is rotating: earnings strength in industrials and value is carrying the Dow to records while the Nasdaq goes sideways. With yields near 5% and oil near $100, the market is rewarding companies that raise guidance and punishing those that trim it, as Netflix is seeing this morning.

One New Thing

Price-weighted index. The Dow is weighted by share price, not company size, so a few high-priced stocks move it most. A 5% jump in 3M (a lower-weight name than the priciest components) helps, but a single expensive stock can swing the Dow more than a much larger company does in the S&P 500.
