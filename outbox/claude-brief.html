6 · Learning Nugget

Real vs. nominal yields. A nominal Treasury yield = the real yield + expected inflation. When yields rise because the real component is climbing (as now), it signals demand for capital and growth rather than inflation fear — which is precisely why equities can shrug off a spike that would normally hurt, unlike an inflation-driven one.
