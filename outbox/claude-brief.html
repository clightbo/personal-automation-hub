Worth Noting

The Angle

This week the leadership flipped. Thursday's pain was in AI-heavy tech, while Friday's was in the Dow and other cyclical names hit by oil and rate worries. With two different drivers hitting different parts of the market, there was little place to hide, and the safe havens were uneven too: bonds were bid but gold fell. Investors face a market that is sensitive to both the AI trade and the price of oil.

One New Thing

Price-weighted vs. cap-weighted. The Dow is price-weighted: stocks with higher share prices move it more, regardless of company size. The S&P 500 and Nasdaq are cap-weighted, so the largest companies dominate. That is why the Dow can fall 1.2% on a day the Nasdaq barely moves: a few high-priced stocks falling can swing it.
