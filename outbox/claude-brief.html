Learning Nugget

Term premium is the extra yield investors demand to hold a long-term bond instead of rolling short-term bills — compensation for rate and inflation uncertainty over the life of the bond. It's why 30Y yields can march to 2007 highs even when the Fed is cutting or Treasury is buying back debt: the market is pricing risk, not just policy.
