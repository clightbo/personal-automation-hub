5 · One Thing to Sound Smart About

The equity risk premium has essentially vanished. With the 10Y near 5% and the S&P around 21x forward earnings (an earnings yield of ~4.8%), stocks now yield less than the risk-free bond — a negative ERP versus a historical 300–500bp cushion. Translation: you're being paid nothing extra to own equity risk over Treasuries. That's why a bond move, not an earnings miss, is what's driving the tape — and why cash and short-duration Treasuries are a live competitor to stocks for the first time in a generation. The contrarian tell: equal-weight (RSP) is beating cap-weight (SPY) in 2026 (~+15% vs +13.3%) as the Mag-7 stalls — leadership is quietly broadening even as the headline index wobbles.

6 · Learning Nugget

Term premium. A 10-year yield is roughly the average expected short rate over ten years plus a "term premium" — the extra compensation investors demand for locking money up long-term (inflation and supply risk). When yields jump while rate-cut bets don't, it's usually the term premium rising — a signal the bond market wants to be paid more for uncertainty, not just for higher Fed rates.
