Worth Noting

The Angle

The risk is no longer just chips. With energy-driven inflation now leaking into core and the 10-year above 5.3%, the whole market is being asked to justify near-record valuations against a higher discount rate.

One New Thing

Core vs. headline CPI. Headline CPI includes volatile food and energy; core strips them out. Policymakers watch core because it better shows underlying price pressure, so a core beat, as today's, matters more than an oil-driven headline jump.
