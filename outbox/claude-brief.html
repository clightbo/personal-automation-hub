The Angle

Today's real tension is one investors haven't resolved yet: record stock prices sitting next to a 10-year yield grinding back toward its highest level since January. That combination rarely holds for long — something usually gives — and Thursday's Trump-Xi summit is as plausible a trigger as any Fed commentary between now and then.

One New Thing

Strait of Hormuz rerouting: when a producer's pipeline shuts down, it doesn't just cut supply — it changes how the oil moves. Saudi Arabia rerouting crude exports back through the Strait of Hormuz, rather than around it, is itself a market signal: tankers don't take the exposed route unless the diplomatic backdrop supports it, which is part of why oil's war-risk premium is unwinding.
